On Kinotic Cloud, the **Billing & plan** tab of **Organization settings** is where your organization pays for
what it uses. A self-hosted installation does not bill, and the tab says billing is not available there.

## Free trial

A new organization starts on a free trial that needs no payment details. Pick a plan on the Billing tab at any
time: Stripe's Checkout page takes your payment details, and when more than two days of the trial are left, the
first charge waits until the trial ends.

## Plans

Kinotic offers the plans. A plan charges a flat fee each month or year, a price for each unit of usage beyond what
it includes, or both:

- **vCPU** (vCPU-hours): the vCPUs of each of your running microservices, times the hours it runs.
- **Memory** (GB-hours): the gigabytes of memory of each running microservice, times the hours it runs.
- **Data storage** (GB-months): the gigabytes your applications' entity data takes up; a gigabyte kept for a
  whole month is one GB-month.

A microservice is billed from the moment its VM starts to the moment it stops, like a cloud VM. Data storage is
read once an hour. Usage is billed by the hour, and usage during the free trial is never billed. Usage during a
subscription is reported to Stripe even when it is cancelled before the hour is billed.

## Usage, forecast and invoices

The Billing tab shows your organization's daily usage for the last 30 days, the usage of each microservice and
application over those days, grouped into compute and storage, a forecast of the current billing period's cost
from its usage so far and what runs and is stored now, and your last 24 invoices, each with a link to view and pay it at Stripe.
**Manage billing** opens Stripe's Customer Portal, where you change your payment method, download invoices and
cancel your subscription.

## When payment is overdue

When the trial ends with no plan, or a payment fails, payment is due. After 14 days overdue, the organization is
deactivated: its microservices stop, and its sites show a page saying the organization is deactivated. A site
stays up while Kinotic Cloud cannot say within two seconds whether its organization is deactivated. Paying
reactivates it right away, and its microservices start again, except a microservice your last deployment no
longer includes, which stays stopped until you remove or deploy it again.
