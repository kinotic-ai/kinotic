On Kinotic Cloud, the **Billing & plan** tab of **Organization settings** is where your organization pays for
what it uses. A self-hosted installation does not bill, and the tab says billing is not available there.

## Free trial

A new organization starts on a free trial that needs no payment details. Pick a plan on the Billing tab at any
time: Stripe's Checkout page takes your payment details, and when more than two days of the trial are left, the
first charge waits until the trial ends.

## Plans

Kinotic offers the plans. A plan charges a flat fee each month or year, a price for each unit of usage beyond what
it includes, or both:

- **vCPU hours**: the vCPUs of your organization's running microservice and build VMs, times the hours they run.
- **Memory GB hours**: the gigabytes of memory of those VMs, times the hours they run.

Usage is measured every five minutes and billed by the hour. Usage during the free trial is never billed.

## Usage, forecast and invoices

The Billing tab shows your organization's daily usage for the last 30 days, a forecast of the current billing
period's cost from its usage so far, and your last 24 invoices, each with a link to view and pay it at Stripe.
**Manage billing** opens Stripe's Customer Portal, where you change your payment method, download invoices and
cancel your subscription.

## When payment is overdue

When the trial ends with no plan, or a payment fails, payment is due. After 14 days overdue, the organization is
deactivated: its microservices stop, and its sites show a page saying the organization is deactivated. Paying
reactivates it right away, and its microservices start again.
