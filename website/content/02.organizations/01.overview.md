An **organization** is the top-level home for everything you build on Kinotic. It owns its members, the identity providers they sign in with, and its [Apps](/organizations/apps/application-structure/overview).

Organization administrators manage the organization from **Organization settings** in the Kinotic portal.

## Organization setup

- **[Authentication providers](/organizations/authentication-providers/overview)** -- let your members sign in to the portal with your company's identity provider, such as Okta.
- **[Apps](/organizations/apps/application-structure/overview)** -- build, publish, and deploy applications inside the organization.

For how organizations, users, and OIDC configurations work under the hood, see [Organization Management](/platform/organization-management).
